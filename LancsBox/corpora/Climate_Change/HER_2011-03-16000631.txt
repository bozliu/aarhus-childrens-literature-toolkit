 aS MSPs cram in as much as possible before this Parliament rises for the last time next week, they are, in an unusual move for a Wednesday, having an all-day sitting and a slightly later finishing time.  Four pieces of legislation have reached their final stage and are expected to be passed   the Certification of Death Bill, the Public Records Bill, the Local Electoral administration Bill and the Domestic abuse Bill.  There will also be two ministerial statements   one by Education Secretary Michael Russell on higher education funding, the other by Roseanna Cunningham on climate change targets.  They will also squeeze in a Government debate on local government finance and a Member s Business debate on a motion by the SNP s Michael Matheson on Denny town centre regeneration. One committee is sitting. MSPs on the Justice Committee will process issues under subordinate legislation.
