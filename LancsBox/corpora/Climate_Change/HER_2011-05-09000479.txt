 CLIMaTE change protesters will converge on St andrew s House in Edinburgh today to urge the new SNP Government to turn its manifesto pledges into reality.  The party s Holyrood manifesto pledged that Scotland would meet all electricity demand from green sources by 2020.  Today s event will see 25 senior representatives from Scotland s largest civil society coalition, Stop Climate Chaos Scotland, come together to hand in a giant postcard at the government offices.  The action follows a disappointing performance for the Scottish Greens, with just two MSPs returned.
