 JON Moulton, the venture capitalist, has hired a former senior employee of BP to help run his new ?200m green energy fund.  Mr Moulton, chairman of Better Capital, has recruited Vince Julier to co-manage Greensphere Capital.  The Sunday Telegraph unveiled Mr Moulton's plans to launch Greensphere in March, revealing the fund would focus on sustainable energy and infrastructure.  Mr Julier was formerly head of structured finance for Europe, Middle East and africa at BP, with a special focus on renewable energy.  He will work alongside Divya Seshamani, a former Goldman Sachs banker who most recently worked at the infrastructure arm of Singapore's sovereign wealth fund, GIC, to manage the fund. Greensphere will be based in offices on London's St James's Square, with Mr Moulton acting as non-executive chairman.  Fundraising is not yet under way, however, with regulatory approval still pending from the Financial Services authority.  Venture capitalist Moulton hit the headlines again earlier this week when Better Capital was connected to all Saints, the retailer which has been up for sale. However he decided not to join with Goode Partners and Lion Capital in a consortium bid.  He founded and ran alchemy Partners for 12 years until 2009, after which he set up Better Capital, which has investments in Reader's Digest and Santia, the latter being made up of some of the subsidiaries of collapsed support-services group Connaught.
