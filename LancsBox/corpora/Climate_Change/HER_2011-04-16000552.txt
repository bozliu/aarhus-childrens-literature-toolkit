 SCOTTISH and Southern Energy has sold three wind farms, including two in Scotland, but said it still plans to increase its renewable generation north of the Border.  Perth-based SSE, owner of Scottish Hydro Electric, raised ?173.6 million by selling its 30-megawatt Dalswinton and 36.8MW Minsca facilities in Dumfries and Galloway as well as Slieve Divena, with 30MW of capacity, in Northern Ireland.
