Who This Report Is For

Libraries, reading platforms, and curriculum teams that need better title discovery, themed list building, and catalog interpretation than age labels and manual tags alone.

Current Capability

  • Rebuild a transparent children’s literature corpus from the 20-book legacy baseline plus a small expansion layer.
  • Compare books through theme profiles, sentiment trajectories, recurring entities, and semantic neighborhoods.
  • Audit provenance, historical clustering, metadata balance, and processing constraints before making broader claims.
  • Publish the results as reusable figures, tables, README/report surfaces, and release assets across both R and Python.

What It Cannot Yet Do

  • No chapter-level or scene-level story forecasting.
  • No personalized reader model.
  • No validated pedagogy scoring, reading-level scoring, or curriculum suitability labels.
  • No market-success, sales, or literary-quality prediction.

MVP Product Shape

  • A searchable children’s literature dashboard.
  • A similar-title panel for related-book navigation.
  • A theme profile panel for reading-list and shelf design.
  • A sentiment arc viewer for narrative pacing comparison.
  • A corpus bias and provenance audit panel for collection review.

Primary User Workflow

  1. Start from a known title, author, or reading theme.
  2. Inspect neighboring books, theme profiles, character prominence, and sentiment pacing.
  3. Compare titles before building a reading list, recommendation surface, or classroom collection.
  4. Export or reuse the evidence in discovery, curation, and collection-audit workflows.

Roadmap

  • Phase 1: ship the current dashboard MVP using existing similarity, theme, sentiment, entity, and corpus-audit outputs.
  • Phase 2: add institution-facing tooling such as filters, cohort comparison, exportable reading lists, and stronger collection views.
  • Phase 3: add sequence-aware modeling only after chapter-level structure, labels, and evaluation data exist.

Why Not Story Prediction Yet

Story prediction is a different task from similarity, theme profiling, and pacing comparison. It requires sequence data, labels, and evaluation that the current repo does not yet have, so this product should be read as editorial and discovery intelligence rather than a forecasting engine.
