# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We want this repository to be welcoming to researchers, developers, students, and commercial practitioners alike. Respectful, constructive collaboration is the baseline.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and specific in reviews.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and evidence, not people.
- Support reproducibility and honest attribution.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Dismissing contributors for asking basic questions.
- Misrepresenting results, sources, or licenses.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or contributions that violate these expectations.
